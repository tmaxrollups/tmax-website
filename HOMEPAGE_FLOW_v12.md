# TMAX Homepage Flow v12

This revision realigns the Netlify homepage to the proven structure of the current TMAX WordPress homepage while retaining the modern design and updated product facts.

Order:
1. Cycling benefit-led hero
2. TMAX introduction / buyer needs
3. Direct-source value statement
4. Product families
5. Why choose TMAX
6. Free consultation form
7. Our Work: gallery and video together
8. Customer testimonials
9. Dealer call-to-action

Hero messages are buyer-problem/benefit led:
- Feeling the Heat? — Exterior Shades & Screens
- Reclaim Your Space — Roll-Up Garage Doors
- Control Light & Privacy — Interior Shades
- Protect What Matters — Roll-Up Shutters
- Secure Your Business — Commercial Systems

The standalone homepage video section was removed. The video now lives alongside the installation gallery in the Our Work section so photography and motion do not interrupt the content flow directly below the hero.
