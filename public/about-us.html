About TMAX

Houston-built roll-up solutions since 2013

TMAX began working with tubular motors in 2013. Their compact design led us to roll-up garage doors, which preserve valuable ceiling space compared with traditional overhead doors.

Since then, we have expanded into custom exterior and interior shades built around the same proven motor concept. Every garage door and shade is made to order, with a focus on dependable products, fair pricing and responsive customer service.

Our Standards

Product Knowledge from Manufacturing Through Support

TMAX works across the full system—materials, housings, rails, motors, controls, safety components, measurements and installation considerations. That product knowledge is especially important on oversized, non-standard or custom projects.

The goal is simple: recommend the right product, manufacture it correctly, document the important project details and remain available when customers need support.

Our Story

Products Designed to Solve Practical Problems

TMAX focuses on roll-up products that improve how residential and commercial spaces function. Garage doors can free ceiling space, exterior shades can improve patio comfort, screens can help control insects, and shutters can add security and protection.

Today the line includes exterior shades and bug screens, interior shades, Neolux dual shades, roll-up garage doors, shutters, controls and commercial applications.
