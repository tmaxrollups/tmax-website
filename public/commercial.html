How we work

Roll-up systems for security, access and service

TMAX supplies roll-up shutters, doors, shades and service-counter systems for restaurants, retail spaces, garages, storefronts and other commercial properties.

Dimensions, mounting surfaces, power, access, safety requirements and applicable code considerations are reviewed during project planning.

  • Perforated steel curtain — visibility for counters while providing a secure barrier.
  • Smooth motor operation — soft start/stop and manual override options.
  • Custom sizing — field-fit housings and mounting for retrofit or new construction.
  • Code & safety — integrated sensors and compliance with local codes.
  • Finish options — powder coat or anodized finishes for durability.

What we install

Match the system to the business need

Commercial projects vary widely. TMAX can help match the product and control package to the location and intended use.

Service & Counter Openings

Compact roll-up solutions for restaurant, bar, retail, and pass-through service openings. These applications can use smaller guides, slats, brackets, and housings than traditional rolling doors, helping reduce required headroom.

Security Shutters

Motorized shutter systems for storefronts and other areas where after-hours security, compact storage and quiet operation are priorities.

Commercial Roll-Up Doors

Custom roll-up door systems for garages and other commercial access points, sized for the project.

Project-Specific Planning

TMAX reviews mounting, electrical, safety, access, and site conditions so the final system can be coordinated with project requirements.

Motorized Controls

Remote controls, wall controls, keypads, and compatible smart-control options can be selected based on product and use.

Exterior Shades & Screens

Motorized solar-shade and insect-screen options can help make restaurant patios and other commercial outdoor spaces more usable.
