About this product

Shade, screen or privacy—choose the right material for the job

TMAX exterior shade and screen systems are custom-sized for windows, decks, porches, pergolas, patios, and other outdoor structures. Options include Mermet Natté solar-screen fabric in 3%, 5%, or 10% openness, blackout material, sealed-edge bug-screen configurations, and Drop Shades for straightforward solar reduction where full insect sealing is not required.

Hardware is powder-coated, and systems can be hardwired or plugged into a standard outlet depending on the installation. Multi-channel remotes can operate individual shades or groups of shades.

Features

Options for sun, insects, privacy and control

Material, mounting, power and control options are selected for the installation and the result you want.

Motorized Operation

Motorized operation with remote control; systems can be configured so multiple shades operate on separate or shared channels.

Mermet Natté Solar Fabrics

Available in 3%, 5%, and 10% openness for different balances of solar control, visibility, and airflow, plus blackout options.

Bug-Screen Options

TuffScreen and TuffScreen No-See-Um options provide durable insect screening for patios, porches, windows, doors, and retractable applications. Zip-style screen systems retain the screen at the edges to help close gaps around the protected area.

Custom Mounting & Sizing

Recessed and surface-mount systems are sized around the finished dimensions, floor conditions, side tracks, housing clearance and electrical access.

Optional Smart Control

An optional Wi-Fi hub can add app-based remote control, timers, scenes, and compatible Alexa or Google voice control.

Documented Warranty Coverage
