About this product

Reclaim your garage ceiling

TMAX roll-up garage doors eliminate the long overhead tracks used by conventional sectional doors. The door rolls into a compact housing above the garage, leaving ceiling space available for lighting, lifts, storage or a cleaner finished look.

Each door is manufactured to the project dimensions. The 2026 product catalog documents insulated construction with a non-insulated option, powder-coated finishes, 120V power, and motor placement on either the left or right side.

Features

Garage door features and options

The final package is confirmed with your quote and depends on door size and project conditions. Current catalog features include the items below.

Motorized 120V Operation

Stainless-steel tubular motor with standard 120V power; the motor can be installed on either side of the opening.

Remote Control Package

Single-door packages include handheld, wall-mount, and keychain controls; multi-door configurations use multi-channel controls as documented in the catalog.

Safety Sensors

Bumper and infrared safety sensors are available/configurable based on the installation.

HomeLink Compatibility

The current garage-door system is documented as compatible with HomeLink.

Manual Override

Manual override functionality and an override crank handle provide a way to operate the door during a power outage.

Insulated or Non-Insulated Slats

Insulated door material is standard in the current catalog, with a non-insulated option also available.

Product-specific limited warranty — 5 years on motors, 2 years on other parts, and 5 years on the entire system for Zip Shade products. Written terms and exclusions apply.
