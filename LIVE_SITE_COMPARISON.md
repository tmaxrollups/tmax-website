# Live WordPress → Netlify Content Comparison

Reviewed against the current live TMAX Roll Ups site on 2026-08-29.

## Preserved or restored
- Direct-source / without-the-middleman value proposition, rewritten without price guarantees.
- Custom-made product positioning and Houston manufacturing/support identity.
- Exterior shade use across windows, decks, porches, pergolas and patios.
- Sealed-edge insect-screen concept for retractable screen systems.
- Interior shades may be manual or automated; the site no longer treats the entire interior category as motorized.
- Garage-door automatic locking, manual override, safety-system positioning, and nationwide shipping availability.
- Commercial service/counter shutters using compact components and reduced headroom relative to traditional rolling doors.
- Hand-held battery remotes and wall controls on the accessories page.
- Dealer application request for business/service-area context and typical 1–3 business-day follow-up language.
- Awareness of legacy measuring, installation and Dooya Wi-Fi guides on Resources.
- Existing customer testimonials retained; unrelated WordPress template text remains excluded.

## Intentionally superseded
- Old blanket 5-year warranty wording: replaced by the current manufacturer warranty terms.
- Old 2024 catalog: replaced by the supplied 2026 catalog.
- Unsupported hurricane/wind certifications and numerical performance claims remain excluded.
- WordPress shop/cart structure and $0/non-purchasable product entries are not carried into the lead-generation site.
- Repetitive/awkward 'opening' language was not restored.

## Product framing used
The Netlify site distinguishes solar shades, sealed insect screens, drop shades, blackout options, manual/motorized interior shades, Neolux dual shades, roll-up garage doors, security/storm shutters, commercial service/security closures, and controls/accessories by function instead of presenting all TMAX products as one motorized category.
